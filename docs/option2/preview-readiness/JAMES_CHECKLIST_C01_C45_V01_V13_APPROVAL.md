## 1. Executive Notice & Regulatory Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Observation Aid Disclaimer:** This checklist is an observation and due-diligence aid designed to structure visual appraisals during property viewings and video walkthrough reviews. It is **not** a building survey, structural appraisal, or statutory compliance certificate, and must **not** be used as a substitute for professional inspections by qualified specialists (including RICS chartered surveyors, Gas Safe registered engineers, NICEIC/NAPIT registered electricians, structural engineers, or UKAS accredited environmental testing laboratories).

### Key Governance & Verification Notes
1. **Independent Authoring & Remediation:** All 58 items were rewritten by engineering to replace historical third-party wording. Automated phrase-matching checks against previously flagged third-party phrases (e.g. Martin Rapley / Refurbishment Mastery 2021) and standard RICS Home Survey titles were conducted across tracked repository files to remove known external strings.
2. **Legal Clearance Limitation:** Technical phrase searches provide code hygiene only and **do not constitute a definitive legal copyright or IP clearance**. Final determination of terminology, regulatory compliance, and copyright adoption rests exclusively with James Lake.
3. **Conditional Defect Formulation:** All observation prompts describe observable physical phenomena without diagnosing concealed causes, mandating repair methods, or certifying statutory compliance. Defect diagnoses and scope estimates remain conditional on qualified professional verification.
4. **Inspection Mode Distinction:** Each item explicitly distinguishes findings available via video walkthrough evidence from those requiring physical in-person operational testing or verbal/documentary inquiry.
5. **Draft Watermarking:** Until James explicitly approves this wording in writing, all user-facing selectors, cards, and modal dialogs in the application display `(Unapproved Working Draft)`. Every item below is marked **PENDING JAMES APPROVAL**.
